What the information on this website is, and what it is not.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Prices are guidance, not quotations

The ranges published on our price guide and elsewhere on this site are indicative of what locksmith work typically costs in the Boksburg area. They exist so you can tell a fair quote from a bad one. They are not a quotation, not an offer, and not binding on us.

The price for your job is the one given to you on the phone and confirmed on site before work begins. It varies with the lock or vehicle involved, the time of day, the difficulty of the job and your location.

Response times are estimates

Where we describe how quickly we can reach you, we are describing what is typical in normal conditions. Traffic, weather, load-shedding, distance and how many jobs are running at that moment all affect it. Nothing on this website guarantees a specific arrival time. If timing is critical, say so on the phone and we will tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

Content is general information

Our guides on locks, keys, security upgrades and what to do in a lockout are general information written for a South African audience. They are not a security assessment of your particular property, and they are not legal, insurance or engineering advice. Do not rely on them as a substitute for having someone actually look at your situation.

Nothing here should be read as instruction on how to defeat a lock you do not own. We attend lockouts only on proof that you are entitled to access the property or vehicle.

Accuracy and availability

We try to keep this site accurate and current, but we do not warrant that every detail is free of error or that the site will always be available. Services, coverage and pricing can change without the website being updated the same day. If something matters to your decision, confirm it with us on 079 106 5911.

Where we link to a third-party website we do so because we think it is useful. We do not control those sites and we are not responsible for their content, their accuracy or how they handle your information.
