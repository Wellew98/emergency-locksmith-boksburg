Referral Marketing Disclosure

We think you should know how this service is arranged before you call, not after someone is standing at your door. This page explains it plainly.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

How this works

Locksmith Boksburg is a locksmith referral and dispatch service. When you call 079 106 5911, we take the details of your job and arrange for a locksmith to attend. The work itself may be carried out by an independent locksmith we work with, rather than by an employee of this website.

We are not a directory, and we do not sell your details on to whoever pays the most for them. We take the job, we arrange the locksmith, and we stay responsible to you for it.

What that means for you

You will know who is coming

Ask on the phone and we will tell you the name of the locksmith attending. You are entitled to know who is at your door before you open it.

The price is agreed before work starts

You get a quote on the phone and it is confirmed on site before anything is drilled, cut or opened. If the job turns out to be different from what was described, the attending locksmith must tell you and re-quote before continuing.

You are not obliged to proceed

If you are unhappy with the price or the person who arrives, you can decline. Any call-out charge that applies will have been disclosed to you on the phone.

Complaints still come to us

If something goes wrong we do not hand you off. Raise it with us on 079 106 5911 and we will deal with it under our Complaints Policy.

Why we publish this

A lot of locksmith call-centre operations in South Africa present themselves as a local workshop with a physical address they do not have, and do not tell you that the person attending is a contractor. We would rather say it up front.

We are a mobile service. We do not operate a walk-in shop or a staffed office in Boksburg — we come to you. Where our pages describe coverage of a suburb, that means we dispatch to that suburb, not that we have premises in it.

Pricing on this website

The figures on our Boksburg locksmith price guide are indicative market ranges to help you judge whether a quote is fair. They are not a quotation and not an offer. Your price depends on the job, the lock or vehicle, the time of day and where you are. See our Disclaimer.
