Repair it or replace it? Usually the honest answer is repair

The typical Boksburg South call is not a break-in. It is a key that has needed a jiggle for two years and this morning it stopped turning altogether, or a mortice lock in a wooden door that has swollen through a wet summer.

A worn cylinder, a dropped door, a gate that needs lifting to lock. Those are usually repairs, and a repair is a fraction of a replacement. Where replacement is genuinely the answer is when the lock is obsolete and no key blank matches it, when the case itself is cracked, or when you simply do not know who else holds a key.

Ask which one it is and expect a straight answer. If somebody looks at a sticking lock in an old door and immediately quotes you for a full replacement plus a new handle set, get a second opinion before you agree.
