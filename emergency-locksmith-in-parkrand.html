The 07:20 call: keys locked in the car at the school gate

This is the most predictable job in Parkrand. Engine running, hazards on, child already out of the car, doors locked with the keys inside. It happens at drop-off far more than at any other hour, and the reason is always the same. The routine is automatic and something interrupted it.

If it happens to you, do not break the window on principle. Opening a modern car without damage is routine work if the person doing it knows the vehicle. Tell us the make, model and year, whether the engine is running, and whether anyone is inside. A child or a pet in a locked car changes the priority completely and you should say so in the first sentence.

The prevention is dull and it works: one spare key, kept somewhere other than the handbag that goes into the locked car with everything else.
