Who is actually allowed to change that lock?

This is where Beyers Park calls go wrong, and it has nothing to do with the locksmithing. In a sectional title unit the front door is usually yours, but the gate, the boom, the common-area doors and often the door frame are not. Change the wrong thing and you are arguing with the body corporate afterwards.

The short version: opening a door you are locked out of is never a problem. Replacing or rekeying a lock is fine if you own the unit. If you rent, get the landlord or managing agent to say yes. A WhatsApp message is enough, and it takes two minutes. If it is a gate motor, a boom remote or an access fob for the complex, that is a trustee or managing agent decision, not yours.

Tell us which of those it is when you call. It changes what we bring and it stops you paying for work you were not entitled to authorise.
