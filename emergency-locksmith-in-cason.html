If it is a break-in, phone the police before you phone us

Cason is close enough to the Fourth Street station that this is worth saying plainly. If your door has been forced, or a lock has been drilled or snapped by somebody else, report it first and get a case number.

The reason is not paperwork for its own sake. Your insurer will almost certainly want the case number and will often want to see the damaged lock before it is replaced, so do not let anybody throw the old hardware away. And once we secure the door, the scene is changed.

The right order is: get somewhere safe, phone the police, phone us. We can board up or secure a forced door the same night and fit the permanent lock once the claim is moving.
