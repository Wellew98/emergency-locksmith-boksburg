Before we open anything

For every lockout we ask for ID and something that shows you are entitled to be there: a lease, a utility bill, a vehicle licence disc, or the registration papers. It is not us being difficult. A locksmith who opens doors without asking is a locksmith who will open your door for someone else too.

If you cannot produce it right then, tell us on the phone and we will talk through what will work. We would rather sort that out before we drive out than argue about it at your gate.
