Dawn Park, the Southern End of Boksburg

Dawn Park is the furthest south we go in Boksburg proper, down past the R554 with Vosloorus about 5 km to the west. Dawn Park Shopping Centre on Galahad Road is the centre of it, SAPS Dawn Park is on Hassink Highway, and the newer estates like Dawn City sit further out towards North Boundary Road.

We cover the established streets and the new developments, including the Methodist church end of South Boundary Road and the Klippoortjie side.

Mobile service, no shop to drive to. Call 079 106 5911 or send a WhatsApp. A location pin is worth more than a street name out here.

Be honest about the distance, and we will be too

Dawn Park is a real drive from central Boksburg, and anyone who tells you otherwise on the phone at midnight is telling you what you want to hear. We will give you an honest estimate when you call, based on where the vehicle actually is at that moment, not a number printed on a web page.

That cuts both ways. If you are locked out at night with children or you are on your own, say so, because it changes what we prioritise. And if the job can wait until morning, such as a lock that still works but is failing, or a spare key you have been meaning to cut, booking it for daylight is cheaper than an after-hours call-out. The price guide shows how big that gap is.

In the newer estates, the other half of the delay is the gate. Warn the guardhouse that a locksmith is coming, or arrange for someone to meet us at the boom.
