What Does a Locksmith Cost in Boksburg?

This is the question everybody actually searches for, and almost every locksmith website in South Africa refuses to answer it. Here are the typical market ranges, so you know roughly what you are in for before you dial anyone.

House lockout

R400 – R1 500 daytime
R900 – R3 300 after hours

Car lockout

R350 – R900
Plus call-out where applicable

Car key replacement

R550 – R2 500 transponder
R2 000 – R4 950 smart key

The after-hours band is wide because South African locksmiths price night work two completely different ways: some add a R200 to R500 surcharge, others charge a flat emergency tariff of R2 200 to R3 600. That difference is worth more than the job itself, and you are entitled to ask which one you are being quoted on.

What People Ask Us

Do you really work 24 hours?

Yes. Lockouts do not keep office hours and neither do we. Call and we will tell you honestly how soon we can be there rather than promising a number we cannot keep.

Can you help if my keys are locked in the car?

Yes. Vehicle lockouts and replacement car keys for most makes, cut and programmed on site. Dealership pricing is usually several times higher for the same key.

Will you damage my door?

Non-destructive entry is always the first approach and most residential locks survive it. If yours cannot be opened that way, you will be told before we start, not after.

What do you need from me?

ID and something showing you are entitled to be there. If that is inside the house you are locked out of, say so on the phone and we will work out what will do instead.

When you call, have this ready

Four things decide how fast we reach you and how accurate the price is. Having them to hand means we can quote you properly on the phone instead of guessing and revising it at your gate.

Where you are, precisely

Suburb, street, and a cross street or landmark. Boksburg is harder to navigate than it looks: numbered streets in Boksburg North, repeating street names across the Vosloorus extensions, and booms and guardhouses across Sunward Park. A nearby shop or school beats a GPS pin every time.

What the lock is

House door, security gate, car, safe, roller door. If you can photograph it, WhatsApp us the picture. It tells us more in one second than two minutes of description.

Make, model and year, for cars

This is the single thing that decides a car key price. A basic transponder key and a smart key for a newer vehicle are two different jobs at two very different prices.

Whether it can wait until morning

If it can, say so. After-hours pricing in Boksburg is where bills get out of hand, and waiting until business hours is often the cheapest decision available to you. We will tell you if waiting makes sense.

Before we open anything

For every lockout we ask for ID and something that shows you are entitled to be there: a lease, a utility bill, a vehicle licence disc, or registration papers. It is not us being difficult. A locksmith who opens doors without asking is a locksmith who will open your door for someone else too.

If you cannot produce it right then, say so on the phone. There is usually a way through it, and it is far better sorted out before we drive out than argued about at your gate.
