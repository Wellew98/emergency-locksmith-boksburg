Almost every locksmith website in South Africa answers the price question with “it depends”. That is not useful when you are standing outside your house at eleven at night. Two ways locksmiths price after-hours work

This is the single biggest reason two quotes for the same midnight lockout can differ by two thousand rand, and almost nobody explains it.

The surcharge model. The locksmith charges the normal rate and adds a night, weekend or public-holiday fee on top. Published South African figures put that at roughly R200 to R500. A daytime house lockout at R900 becomes about R1 100 to R1 400 at midnight.

The emergency tariff model. After-hours work is priced as a separate service altogether, with published bands running from about R2 200 to R3 600 regardless of how simple the job turns out to be. Under this model the same lockout is R2 500 whether it takes four minutes or forty.

Both are legitimate and both are openly published by South African locksmiths. But you are entitled to know which one you are being quoted on before anyone gets in a vehicle. Ask directly: “Is that your normal rate plus a call-out, or an emergency rate?”

And if the job can wait until morning, wait. A spare key, a lock you want changed, a gate motor remote: none of that needs an after-hours call-out. It is the cheapest decision available to you.

What actually moves the price

Time of day

The single biggest factor. The same lockout can cost two to three times more at 2am than at 2pm. If the job can wait until morning, it is worth waiting.

Destructive vs non-destructive entry

If the lock can be picked or bypassed you pay labour only. If it must be drilled, you are also paying for replacement hardware.

Key type

A plain mechanical key is cheap to cut. A transponder key needs programming equipment. A proximity or smart key for a recent luxury vehicle is the most expensive category by a wide margin.

Distance

Call-out fees scale with travel. A locksmith already working in Boksburg will usually quote lower than one driving from Johannesburg.

Common questions about locksmith pricing

How much is the cheapest locksmith in Boksburg?

The cheapest jobs are key duplication and simple rekeying, which generally start around R250. Be careful with quotes far below the ranges on this page. Unusually low call-out fees are sometimes followed by a much larger bill once the technician arrives. Ask for the total, including call-out, before anyone is dispatched.

How much does a locksmith cost in South Africa?

Published rates across South Africa generally fall between R250 and R3 500, with most routine residential jobs landing between R400 and R1 500. Gauteng tends to sit at the higher end of that band compared with coastal cities. Emergency and after-hours work is consistently the most expensive category.

How much does it cost to change a lock?

A standard door lock replacement usually runs R550 to R1 500 including the hardware. Rekeying the existing lock is cheaper at R250 to R1 200 and is often the better choice if the lock itself is undamaged and you simply want old keys to stop working.

What locks can locksmiths not open?

Very few locks are genuinely impossible, but some cannot be opened without damage. High-security cylinders with anti-drill and anti-pick protection, certain safes with relocking devices, and some modern vehicle immobilisers may require destructive entry or manufacturer involvement. A locksmith should tell you before starting if non-destructive entry is unlikely.

Why is an after-hours locksmith more expensive?

South African locksmiths use two different models for this, and the gap between them is large. Some add a night, weekend or public-holiday surcharge of roughly R200 to R500 on top of the normal rate. Others price after-hours work as a separate emergency tariff, with published bands from about R2 200 to R3 600 regardless of how simple the job is. Ask which one you are being quoted on. If the job is not urgent, waiting until business hours can halve the bill.

Do locksmiths charge a call-out fee even if they can't help?

Usually yes. The call-out covers travel and time, so it is normally payable whether or not the job is completed. Confirm this on the phone before dispatch, along with what happens if the lock turns out to need replacing rather than opening.
