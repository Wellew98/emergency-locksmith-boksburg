Locksmith FAQ — Boksburg

The questions Boksburg customers ask most often, answered directly. For a full breakdown of what jobs cost, see our Boksburg locksmith price guide.

How much is the cheapest locksmith in Boksburg?

The cheapest jobs are key duplication and simple rekeying, which generally start around R250. Be careful with quotes far below the ranges on this page — unusually low call-out fees are sometimes followed by a much larger bill once the technician arrives. Ask for the total, including call-out, before anyone is dispatched.

How much does a locksmith cost in South Africa?

Published rates across South Africa generally fall between R250 and R3 500, with most routine residential jobs landing between R400 and R1 500. Gauteng tends to sit at the higher end of that band compared with coastal cities. Emergency and after-hours work is consistently the most expensive category.

How much does it cost to change a lock?

A standard door lock replacement usually runs R550 to R1 500 including the hardware. Rekeying the existing lock is cheaper at R250 to R1 200 and is often the better choice if the lock itself is undamaged and you simply want old keys to stop working.

What locks can locksmiths not open?

Very few locks are genuinely impossible, but some cannot be opened without damage. High-security cylinders with anti-drill and anti-pick protection, certain safes with relocking devices, and some modern vehicle immobilisers may require destructive entry or manufacturer involvement. A locksmith should tell you before starting if non-destructive entry is unlikely.

Why is an after-hours locksmith more expensive?

Night, weekend and public-holiday call-outs carry a surcharge, typically R200 to R500 on top of normal rates, and some operators price emergency work as a separate higher tariff altogether. If the situation is not urgent, waiting until business hours can cut the bill substantially.

Do locksmiths charge a call-out fee even if they can't help?

Usually yes. The call-out covers travel and time, so it is normally payable whether or not the job is completed. Confirm this on the phone before dispatch, along with what happens if the lock turns out to need replacing rather than opening.
