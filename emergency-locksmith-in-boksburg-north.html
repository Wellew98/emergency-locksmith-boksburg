A Locksmith Who Knows the Numbered Streets

Boksburg North is the old grid immediately north of the Boksburg CBD, and almost none of it is named. It is Second Street, Fourth Street, Sixth Street, Tenth Street, running one into the next. We work it every week, so you do not have to describe it twice.

We cover the whole suburb: the blocks around Fourth Street near the police station and Christ Church, the stretch of Tenth Street past Jericho Road Bible Church and Junior Oxford, Sixth Street by Laerskool Goudrand, and out along Paul Kruger Street, Span Avenue, Cason Road and Trichardt Road. If you are near Rainbow Mica on Cason Road, or heading up towards East Rand Mall about ten minutes west, we know where you mean.

This is a mobile service. There is no shop to drive to. You call, we come out, and most jobs finish on the spot at the vehicle. Call 079 106 5911 or send a WhatsApp; a photo of the lock usually beats describing it.

“I’m on Fourth Street” is not enough

This is the one thing that slows a call-out down in Boksburg North. Numbered streets are not unique. There is a Fourth Street here, and there are numbered streets in other parts of Boksburg and across the East Rand. Drop that into a GPS at eleven at night and there is a real chance we end up in the wrong suburb entirely.

So give us the number and the nearest cross street. “Fourth Street, near the police station.” “Tenth Street, by the church.” “Sixth, opposite the laerskool.” That one extra detail is the difference between us arriving once and us phoning you back twice from the wrong corner.

If you are not sure of the street at all, a landmark works just as well: a school, a church, a shop, the hardware on Cason Road.

Before we open anything

For every lockout we ask for ID and something showing you are entitled to be there: a lease, a utility bill, a vehicle licence disc, or registration papers. It is not us being difficult. A locksmith who opens doors without asking is a locksmith who will open your door for someone else too.

If you cannot produce it right then, say so on the phone and we will talk through what will work. Better to sort that out before we drive out than argue about it at your gate.
