What to have ready when you call

Having these to hand means we can quote you accurately over the phone instead of guessing:

Where you are

The suburb and street, or the shopping centre or complex name. Boksburg has a lot of similar street names across Boksburg North, South and Sunward Park — the more specific, the faster we find you.

What kind of lock

Mortice, cylinder, gate lock, security gate, safe. A photo on WhatsApp is quicker than describing it.

For car keys: make, model and year

This decides whether it's a simple cut or a transponder that needs programming — and it's the single biggest factor in the price. See the Boksburg price guide for the ranges.

Whether you can prove it's yours

For any lockout we ask for ID and proof you're entitled to access the property or vehicle. It protects you as much as us, so please have it ready.
