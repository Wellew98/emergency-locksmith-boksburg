If you live in a complex, sort the gate out first

This is the Sunward Park problem, and almost nobody warns you about it. Most of this suburb is behind a boom or a guardhouse, and the security at those gates is strict, which is exactly why people move here. It also means a locksmith cannot simply arrive at your door.

The trap is that if you are locked out of your own house, your phone, your access card and the guardhouse number are often on the wrong side of that door. So when you call us, tell us up front that you are in a complex, and give us the complex name and street. Then, while we are on the way, get word to the gate that you are expecting a locksmith. A neighbour, the estate WhatsApp group, or the managing agent will do it in a minute.

If the gate will not let us in without the owner present and you are the tenant, say so on the phone. There is usually a way through it, but it is far easier to solve before we are parked outside your boom at midnight.

Before we open anything

For every lockout we ask for ID and something showing you are entitled to be there: a lease, a utility bill, a vehicle licence disc, or registration papers. It is not us being difficult. A locksmith who opens doors without asking is a locksmith who will open your door for someone else too.

If you cannot produce it right then, say so on the phone and we will talk through what will work. Better to sort that out before we drive out than argue about it at your gate.
