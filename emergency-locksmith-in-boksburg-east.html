Boksburg East, and Why Directions Matter Here

Boksburg East is a residential pocket on the eastern edge of Boksburg, out past the CBD towards the Parkrand and Sunward Park side. The nearest landmark most people would recognise is Parkrand Piazza, roughly 2.5 km away on Van Wyk Louw Drive, and the CBD is about the same distance west.

That is the honest description. Boksburg East has no mall of its own and no obvious anchor, which is precisely why the first thing we ask for is your street and your nearest cross street.

Mobile service, no shop to drive to. Call 079 106 5911 or send a WhatsApp. A dropped pin gets us there faster than an address.

Send a pin, not a description

Half the delay on a call-out in an area like this is not the drive. It is the last two minutes: a road name that repeats elsewhere in Boksburg, a plot number that is not on the wall, a gate that looks like the four gates either side of it.

So when you call, do three things. Drop us a WhatsApp location pin. Tell us what the gate looks like: colour, whether it is a driveway gate or a pedestrian gate, whether there is a dog. And if you are standing outside in the dark, tell us that too, because it changes the order we do things in.

If you have a security company patrolling your street, let them know a locksmith is coming. It saves an awkward conversation with an armed response vehicle while we are working on your lock at midnight.
