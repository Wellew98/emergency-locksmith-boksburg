The four calls Boksburg homes actually make

Locked out

Door pulled shut, key snapped, only set inside. Opened non-destructively wherever the lock allows, so you keep the hardware you have. Emergency lockout →

New keys, new house

You have just moved in and you have no idea how many keys are in circulation — previous owner, tenants, an agent, a domestic worker, a builder. Rekeying resets all of it.

A lock that has been getting worse for months

The key needs a lift, a jiggle, a particular angle. That is wear, and it ends with you standing outside. Usually a repair rather than a replacement.

Gates, trellises and burglar bars

Security gate cylinders that jam, gate locks that only close if you lift the gate, burglar bar locks whose keys went missing years ago.

Rekey or replace? Usually rekey

This is where most people are overcharged, so it is worth understanding before anyone quotes you.

Rekeying keeps your existing lock and changes its internals so it answers to a new key and ignores every old one. The hardware on your door stays. It is the right answer when the lock works fine and the problem is who holds keys — after a move, a tenant leaving, a break-up, a lost bunch.

Replacement is the right answer when the lock itself is the problem: the case is cracked, the cylinder is worn round, the lock is obsolete and no blank matches it, or it was damaged in a forced entry. It is also right when you are deliberately upgrading to something stronger.

If somebody looks at a sticking lock in a sound door and immediately quotes a full replacement plus a new handle set, get a second opinion. Ask which one they are recommending and why, and expect a straight answer.

What home locksmith work costs in Boksburg

Market ranges compiled from published South African sources, so you can judge a quote before you accept it:

  • Call-out fee: R350 – R750
  • Rekey a lock: R250 – R1 200
  • Standard lock replacement: R550 – R1 500
  • House lockout, daytime: R400 – R1 500
  • House lockout, after hours: R900 – R3 300
  • High-security lock, supplied and fitted: R1 200 – R3 500 each

The daytime and after-hours gap is the one that catches people. If the job can wait until morning — a failing lock, a spare key, a rekey after a tenant moves out — book it for daylight and pay the lower end.

What happens when we arrive

ID and proof of address first — a lease, a municipal bill, a bond statement. Then the lock is assessed and you are told what it needs and what it costs before any work starts, including whether a repair will genuinely hold or is only buying a few months.

If you rent, clear a lock change with your landlord or managing agent before we come out. Opening a door you are locked out of is never a problem; changing a lock on someone else's property without permission is a different conversation, and it is easier had in advance.
