Commissioner Street and the Boksburg CBD

Commissioner Street is the spine of Boksburg Central. Shoprite, PEP, Ackermans and Mr Price all trade off it, the uniform shops sit at the 275–279 Commissioner Street end, and the Civic Centre and cenotaph are on the Bank Street corner.

We cover the CBD block by block: Commissioner Street, Market Street, Leeuwpoort Street past Boksburg High School and the old library, Osborne Street by the Methodist church, and the flats and offices in between.

Mobile service across the CBD. Call 079 106 5911 or WhatsApp us. Send a photo of the lock or the shutter and we will know what to bring.

Trading hours are exactly when you cannot afford a locked door

The CBD problem is timing. A jammed roller shutter at 07:30 on a Saturday is not an inconvenience, it is a morning of trade gone. A shopfront that will not lock at 18:00 means somebody sleeps in the shop or the stock goes home in a bakkie.

The commercial locks along Commissioner Street take a beating: shutter locks full of street grit, cylinders worn round by ten years of staff keys, padlocks that have been cut and replaced with whatever was in the drawer. Most of what we do here is not a dramatic lockout. It is a lock that has been getting harder to turn for months and finally stopped.

If you run a shop or an office in the CBD, the cheap version of this call is the one you make before it fails. Sticking key, shutter that needs a lift to lock, a cylinder that only works one way. That is a repair now and a replacement later.

Before we open anything

For business premises we ask for something linking you to the address: a lease, a trading licence, a utility account, or the owner on the phone. For a vehicle, the licence disc or registration papers.

It is a short conversation and it protects the next shop owner as much as it protects you. If the SAPS station on Commissioner Street is already involved, say so and bring the case number.
