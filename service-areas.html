Locksmith Service Areas in Boksburg

We are a mobile locksmith service. There is no shop to drive to — the vehicle and the equipment come to your house, your gate, your business or wherever the car is standing, across the whole of Boksburg, twenty-four hours a day.

Twelve suburbs have their own page below, each written around what actually goes wrong there rather than a description of how pleasant the streets are. If your suburb is not listed, call anyway. The list is where we have written things up, not the edge of where we go.

Call 079 106 5911 or send a WhatsApp — a photo of the lock and a location pin get you a straighter answer than a description will.

Where we work, and what each suburb calls us for

Boksburg North

The numbered-street grid off Fourth and Sixth. Tell us the street and the cross street — numbered streets repeat across the East Rand and a pin at 11pm lands in the wrong suburb.

Sunward Park

The Kingfisher Avenue corridor, the Lifestyle Centre, and Netcare Sunward Park Hospital. Mostly complexes behind booms, so clear gate access while we are on the way.

Vosloorus

Chris Hani Mall, Gold Spot, Lesedi City and the extensions. Give us your extension number — it is the single thing that decides whether we find you quickly.

Boksburg Central

The CBD along Commissioner Street. Shopfronts, roller shutters, offices and cars in street parking.

Beyers Park

Estate and sectional-title country off Davidson, Kirschner and Tenth. Check who is allowed to change the lock before we come out.

Parkrand

Around Parkrand Piazza and the two schools. The most predictable job here is keys locked in the car at drop-off.

Atlasville

Atlas Mall, Summerfields, and the guest houses on the OR Tambo boundary. Keys that flew out of the country are our speciality here.

Dawn Park

Galahad Road, the newer estates and the Klippoortjie side. The furthest south we go, so ask for an honest ETA when you call.

Boksburg South

Established housing and older hardware. Worn cylinders and mortice locks in wooden doors more than anything else.

Boksburg West

Where the houses run into workshops and yards. Padlocks, gate locks and rekeying after staff turnover.

Boksburg East

A residential pocket with no obvious landmark. Send a WhatsApp pin rather than a street name.

Cason

The Cason Road strip beside Boksburg North. If a lock has been forced, get the case number from the Fourth Street station first.

How far we travel, and what it costs

Boksburg is bigger than it looks on a map, and Dawn Park to Atlasville is a real drive. Anyone who promises you a fixed arrival time at midnight without asking where you are is telling you what you want to hear.

What you will get from us is an honest estimate on the call, based on where the vehicle actually is at that moment. And if the job can wait for daylight — a failing lock, a spare key, a rekey after a tenant moves out — booking it for the morning costs you meaningfully less than an after-hours call-out.
